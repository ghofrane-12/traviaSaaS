2.2. PERSONAL ITEM POLICIES
---
Most airlines allow **1 personal item** (e.g., laptop bag, purse, small backpack) **in addition** to your cabin bag.
**Exceptions:**
- **Ryanair/EasyJet:** Personal item must fit under the seat (40x20x25 cm).
- **United/Delta:** Personal item can be slightly larger (e.g., small backpack).
- **Emirates/Qatar:** Allow a **laptop bag + small purse** as personal items.

> **Real-Life Scenario: Personal Item Rejected on Ryanair**
> **Situation:** A traveler’s **small backpack (42x22x25 cm)** was rejected as a personal item on Ryanair.
> **Solution:**
> 1. Paid **€50** to check the backpack.
> 2. Later disputed the fee and received a **€25 refund** by emailing Ryanair customer service.
> **Lesson:** **Always measure your personal item**—Ryanair’s sizers are **unforgiving**.

---
2.3. LAPTOP/ELECTRONICS POLICIES
---
- **All airlines** require laptops/tablets to be **removed from bags** during security screening.
- **Power banks** must be in **carry-on only** (never in checked luggage).
- **E-cigarettes/vapes** must be in **carry-on** (fire risk in hold).
- **Spare lithium batteries** must be in **original packaging** (max 2 per passenger).

> **Pro Tip:**
> - **Use a TSA-approved laptop bag** (e.g., with a separate compartment) to speed up security.
> - **Label your electronics** with your contact info in case of loss.

4. PROHIBITED & RESTRICTED ITEMS
===============================================================================
---
4.1. GLOBAL PROHIBITED ITEMS (CABIN & CHECKED BAGGAGE)
---
**Never Pack These (Confiscation Risk):**
- **Weapons:** Guns, knives, box cutters, pepper spray.
- **Explosives:** Fireworks, flares, dynamite.
- **Flammable Items:** Lighter fluid, paint, gasoline.
- **Sharp Objects:** Scissors (>6 cm), razor blades, swords.
- **Tools:** Hammers, drills, saws (>7 inches).
- **Compressed Gases:** Propane, butane, aerosol cans (>100ml).
- **Lithium Batteries:** **Never in checked luggage** (fire risk). Carry in cabin only.

---
4.2. COUNTRY-SPECIFIC RESTRICTIONS
---
| Country/Region     | Unique Prohibited Items                          | Notes                                  |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| USA                | **Powders >12 oz** (e.g., protein powder).      | TSA may require extra screening.      |
| UK                 | **Knives of any size** (even plastic).          | Strict liquid rules (100ml max).      |
| Australia          | **Fresh food, seeds, wooden items**.            | **$1,100+ fines** for undeclared items.|
| UAE/Dubai          | **E-cigarettes in checked luggage**.             | **4-year jail term** for drug residues. |
| Singapore          | **Chewing gum** (illegal to import).            | **$1,000 fine** for possession.       |
| Japan              | **Many common medications** (e.g., Adderall).   | Requires prior approval.               |
| China              | **VPNs, religious materials**.                  | **Confiscation + possible detention**. |
| EU (Schengen)      | **Meat/dairy from outside EU**.                 | **€200+ fines** for undeclared items. |

> **Real-Life Scenario: Confiscated Protein Powder in USA**
> **Situation:** A traveler’s **1kg protein powder** was confiscated at **LAX security** because it exceeded the **12 oz (340g) powder limit**.
> **Solution:**
> 1. **Shipped the powder ahead** via FedEx ($30).
> 2. **Filed a TSA complaint** and received a **$25 voucher** for the inconvenience.
> **Lesson:** **Check TSA’s powder rules** before packing supplements. **Pre-ship** if necessary.

---
4.3. LIQUID RULES (2026 UPDATES)
---
- **Standard Rule:** Containers ≤100ml in a **1L clear, resealable bag** (1 bag per passenger).
- **Exceptions:**
  - **Medications/baby food:** Allowed in reasonable quantities (>100ml).
  - **Duty-free liquids:** Must be in **sealed, tamper-evident bags**.
  - **EU/UK:** All liquids must be **removed from hand luggage** for screening.
- **Country Variations:**
  - **USA:** Strict **3-1-1 rule** (3.4 oz containers, 1 quart bag, 1 bag per passenger).
  - **Australia:** **Aerosols limited to 50ml**.
  - **Japan:** **No liquid restrictions** for domestic flights.

> **Pro Tip:**
> - **Use travel-sized containers** (e.g., **GoToob** leak-proof bottles).
> - **Pack liquids in an easy-to-access pocket** for security screening.
> - **Buy toiletries after security** if traveling with only cabin baggage.

6. LOST/DAMAGED BAGGAGE: WHAT TO DO
===============================================================================
---
6.1. IMMEDIATE STEPS AT THE AIRPORT
---
1. **Report to the airline’s baggage service desk** before leaving the airport.
2. **File a Property Irregularity Report (PIR)**—get a **reference number**.
3. **Keep your baggage receipts** (required for claims).
4. **Ask for essentials reimbursement** if baggage is delayed >24h (e.g., toiletries, clothes).
5. **Take photos** of damaged baggage (for insurance claims).

---

---

> **Pro Tip:**
> - **Save receipts** for essential purchases (e.g., clothes, toiletries)—most airlines reimburse **$50–$100/day**.

8. PACKING TIPS & TRICKS
===============================================================================
---
8.1. HOW TO AVOID EXTRA FEES
---
1. **Weigh your bag at home** (use a **luggage scale**).
2. **Wear your heaviest clothes/shoes** on the plane.
3. **Use compression bags** to maximize space.
4. **Ship heavy items ahead** (e.g., **SendMyBag, Luggage Forward**).
5. **Check airline policies**—some allow **extra weight for students/military**.
6. **Use a soft-sided bag** that can "squish" if slightly oversized.

---
8.2. PACKING FRAGILE ITEMS
---
1. **Wrap items in bubble wrap** and place in the **middle of your bag**.
2. **Use a hard-shell suitcase** for extra protection.
3. **Mark as "Fragile"** on all sides (some airlines provide stickers).
4. **Take photos** before checking in (for insurance claims).
5. **Consider shipping separately** for valuable items (e.g., **FedEx, UPS**).

---
8.3. MAXIMIZING CABIN BAGGAGE SPACE
---
1. **Use packing cubes** to organize items.
2. **Roll clothes** instead of folding to save space.
3. **Wear bulky items** (e.g., winter coat, boots).
4. **Use the "bundle wrapping" method** for wrinkle-free packing.
5. **Pack a foldable tote** for extra space if needed.

> **Pro Tip:**
> - **Use a vacuum bag** for clothes to save **30% more space**.
> - **Pack a collapsible water bottle** to fill after security.
> - **Carry a portable luggage scale** to avoid surprises at check-in.

12. EMERGENCY BAGGAGE SITUATIONS
===============================================================================
---
12.1. LAST-MINUTE OVERWEIGHT BAGGAGE
---
**What to Do:**
1. **Repack items** into your cabin bag or wear extra layers.
2. **Ask the agent for a "courtesy pass"**—some airlines waive fees for loyal customers.
3. **Ship heavy items ahead** (e.g., **FedEx, UPS**).
4. **Pay the fee** if no other option (but **dispute it later**—some airlines refund as a goodwill gesture).

---
12.2. BROKEN BAGGAGE ZIPPER AT CHECK-IN
---
**What to Do:**
1. **Ask the airline for a free plastic wrap** (many airports offer this).
2. **Use duct tape** (carry a small roll in your travel kit).
3. **Request a replacement bag** if the damage is severe (some airlines provide loaners).
4. **File a damage report** before flying (for insurance claims).

---
12.3. LOST BAGGAGE WITH ESSENTIAL ITEMS
---
**What to Do:**
1. **File a PIR (Property Irregularity Report)** at the baggage desk.
2. **Ask for an essentials reimbursement** (most airlines offer **$50–$100/day**).
3. **Buy replacements** (keep receipts for reimbursement).
4. **Use a GPS tracker** (e.g., **AirTag**) to locate your bag.
5. **Contact your travel insurance** if the bag is not found within 5 days.

> **Real-Life Scenario: Lost Baggage with Wedding Dress**
> **Situation:** A traveler’s **checked baggage (with wedding dress)** was lost on a **Lufthansa flight**.
> **Solution:**
> 1. **Filed a PIR** and received a **€100 voucher** for essentials.
> 2. **Rented a replacement dress** (€200, reimbursed by Lufthansa).
> 3. **Bag was found 3 days later**—Lufthansa paid **€500** for dry cleaning.
> **Lesson:** **Never pack irreplaceable items** (e.g., wedding dresses, heirlooms) in checked luggage.
